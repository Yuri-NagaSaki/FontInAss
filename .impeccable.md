## Design Context

### Users
AniBT subtitle makers, subtitle groups, and anime fans process ASS/SSA/SRT subtitles, contribute fonts, share finished archives, and discuss production issues. Group members maintain fonts using workshop credentials; administrators manage indexing and review submissions.

### Brand Personality
Community-minded, calm, precise. This is AniBT’s shared subtitle workshop, powered by the FontInAss engine. User-facing pages lead with AniBT; technical project names, CLI commands, API contracts and credentials keep their existing meaning.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow AniBT’s current cel day/night style, referenced from `AniBT/docs/development/ui-design-spec.md` and `src/styles/app.css` on 2026-09-26. Use the existing whale-girl AniBT logo, identical canvas and paper colors, recessed surfaces, 2px section rules, a restrained cherry accent, pill buttons, and opaque navigation. Zen Maru Gothic Latin 500/700 is paired with the platform CJK body stack; M PLUS 1 Code is reserved for technical values. No gradients, glass effects, emoji decoration, placeholder metrics or generic feature-card walls.

### Design Principles
1. Organize the service around processing, contributing and sharing within the AniBT community.
2. Use a shared service directory, navigation, day/night tokens and localized copy across every route.
3. Preserve real workflows and permission boundaries; never imply shared sign-in that does not exist.
4. Keep action labels specific and copy factual. Explain missing fonts, review requirements and recovery where they matter.
5. Keep every route available on phones, with visible focus, keyboard access, readable contrast and reduced-motion support.
